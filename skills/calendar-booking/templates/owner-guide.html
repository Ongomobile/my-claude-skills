Owner guide

Your booking calendar.
How to run it.

Everything lives in two places: your Google Sheet and your Google Calendar. You never touch the code. Here's what each part does and how to handle the everyday stuff.

What happens when someone books

1. They pickA day and time on your site, shown in their own time zone.
2. It booksAn event lands on your Google Calendar with a Meet link.
3. Everyone hearsGoogle emails them the invite. You get a heads-up email.
4. It's loggedA new row appears on the Bookings tab.

Nobody can grab a time you're busy. The site checks your calendar again the moment they click book.

Where to see your bookings

Google Calendar

Sign in as the account that runs the booking script. Each call shows as "Your name + Their name: Free 30-minute call", with the Meet link and their answers in the description.

The Bookings tab

One row per call: when they booked, name, email, call time, their time zone, what they're working on, website, Meet link and event link. Sort it, filter it, it's your lead list.

How your calendar blocks time

Anything on your calendar that shows as Busy blocks that time, plus the gap around it. A dentist appointment from 1 to 2 with a 30 minute gap closes 12:30 to 2:30.

All-day events don't block anything. Google marks them "Free" by default. To take a whole day off, use the Days off tab. Or open the event and set Show as: Busy.

Want your personal calendar to count too? Share it with your booking account (at least "See only free/busy"), find its Calendar ID in Google Calendar under Settings > that calendar > Integrate calendar, and add it to Calendars to check, separated by a comma: primary, you@gmail.com.

Everyday tasks

Move a call

  1. Drag the event to the new time in Google Calendar.
  2. When Google asks, click Send so they get the update.

Cancel a call

  1. Open the event and click the trash can.
  2. Click Send on the cancellation email.

Take a week off

  1. Add each date to Days off.
  2. Move or cancel anyone already booked that week.

Pause all bookings

  1. Clear the Start and End cells on the Hours tab.
  2. Your site shows "No open times". Put the hours back to reopen.

If a guest declines the invite, Google emails you, same as any meeting.
