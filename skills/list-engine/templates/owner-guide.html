How to run it.

Everything lives in one Google Sheet, {{SHEET_NAME}}. You never touch the code. Here's what each part does, and how to send an email to everyone on your list.

What happens when someone signs up

1. They sign upName and email, on your website.
2. They get the emailStraight away, from {{BRAND}}. Downloads are in it and on the thank-you screen.
3. You hear about itA "New signup" email lands in your inbox.
4. It's loggedA new row appears on the Signups tab.

Reply to the "New signup" email and your reply goes straight to them.

The emails you get

New signup

Who signed up and when. Their welcome email already went out, so there's nothing to do unless you want to say hello.

Unsubscribed

Someone clicked unsubscribe. Their row is marked unsubscribed and list emails skip them from now on. Nothing to do.

Broadcasts

Where you write emails to your whole list. One row per email. How it works is in the next section.

Broadcast log

Written for you. One row for every list email sent: when, which email, and who got it. Handy when someone asks "did I get that?"

Send an email to your whole list

  1. Open the Broadcasts tab and use a new row.
  2. Fill in Subject and Message. Leave a blank line between paragraphs. Don't start with "Hi" or a name: "Hi Jane," is added for you, and so is the unsubscribe link at the bottom.
  3. Want a button? Put its words in Button text and the full web address, starting with https://, in Button link. No button? Leave both empty.
  4. Set Status to test. In about a minute a copy arrives at {{TEST_INBOX}}, with [Test] in the subject. Read it on your phone.
  5. Happy with it? Set Status to send.

Emails go out between {{SEND_HOURS}}, up to {{DAILY_LIMIT}} a day. A bigger list finishes over the next few days by itself. That limit keeps room for the welcome emails new signups need.

What Status tells you

StatusMeaning
draftNothing happens. Work on it as long as you like.

sendingOn its way. Progress says how many have gone and what's next.

errorSomething's missing. Progress says what. Fix it and pick test or send again.

Stop a send partway

  1. Set Status back to draft.
  2. Pick send again later and it carries on. Nobody gets it twice.

Take someone off your list

  1. Find every row with their email on Signups.
  2. Change status to unsubscribed on each one.

Someone asks to join? Send them to the signup form on your website. That records that they asked, and gives them their own unsubscribe link. Don't type people into the Sheet yourself.

Getting into the inbox, not spam

Send every 2 to 4 weeks. A steady rhythm does better than a burst after a long quiet spell.

Keep it short and mostly words, with one button at most. Ask a question people can answer by hitting reply, like "What should we make next?" Replies tell Gmail people want your emails.

If something looks off

You seeTry this
The test didn't arriveCheck spam. Then look at Status: error means Progress says why. Still test after a few minutes? Pick draft, then test again.
Status stays on sendingNormal for a big list or outside sending hours. Progress says when the rest go out.
Progress says PausedThe email service hit a limit or had a hiccup. It tries again within the hour by itself.
One address failedThe Broadcast log shows which. It's usually a typo in their email. Everyone else still got yours.
No "New signup" emailsCheck spam. If new rows still appear on Signups, the signup itself is working.

Leave these alone: the tab names, the header rows, the token column on Signups, the Id column on Broadcasts, and rows on the Broadcast log while a send is going. Share the Sheet with people you trust, never "anyone with the link". Anyone who can edit it can email your whole list.
